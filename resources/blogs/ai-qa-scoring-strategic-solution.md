# AI QA Scoring: Strategic Solution to Improve Contact Center Agent Quality and Performance

## Introduction
In the contact center industry, every agent conversation is not just communication—it's potential revenue or financial risk.
A single script error by a tele sales agent can derail a closing opportunity. A single violation by a tele collection agent can lead to complaints, fines, and reputational risk.
However, in reality, most companies can only monitor a small fraction of their agent conversations. This is where [AI QA Scoring](/en/product/ai-qa-scoring) comes in as an AI-based quality control system to ensure agent performance, script compliance, and conversation quality are maintained consistently and effectively at scale.

## What Is AI QA Scoring for Contact Center
[AI QA Scoring](/en/product/ai-qa-scoring) is an Artificial Intelligence-based Quality Assurance system that automatically analyzes and scores 100% of interactions between contact center agents and customers—whether through phone, chat, or other digital channels.
This system not only provides scores but also deep insights regarding agent performance, script compliance, empathy levels, and potential risks in tele sales and tele collection activities.

## The Limits of Manual QA in Agent Monitoring
Manual QA approaches have serious limitations in modern contact center environments:
- Only 1–5% of agent conversations are analyzed
- Risk of script violations is often detected too late
- Quality assessment is inconsistent due to human bias
- QA teams struggle to scale with growing interaction volumes
As a result, management lacks full visibility into agent performance in the field.

## Risks of Tele Sales and Tele Collection Without Automated QA
Without an automated monitoring system, tele sales and tele collection agent activities carry high risks.
Misinformation, excessive pressure on customers, or regulatory violations can directly impact company revenue and reputation.
[AI QA Scoring](/en/product/ai-qa-scoring) enables early detection of risky conversation patterns before problems escalate further.

## How AI QA Scoring Works in Assessing Agent Performance
[AI QA Scoring](/en/product/ai-qa-scoring) works through the following stages:
1. Automatically collecting all agent conversation data
2. Analyzing conversations using Natural Language Processing (NLP)
3. Providing objective performance and compliance scores
4. Presenting real-time dashboards, performance trends, and risk alerts
